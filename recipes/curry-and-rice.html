Curry and rice

Description

Curry and rice is a uniquely evolved national dish in Japan, made by stewing meat and vegetables with a blend of spices, thickening the sauce with flour, and serving it over white rice

Ingredients

  • 1 box of Curry sauce mix
  • Meat 500g
  • Onions 4
  • Potatoes 3
  • Carrot 1
  • Oil 2 tablespoons
  • Water 1400ml

Steps

  1. Saute ingredients until the onions are lightly browned.
  2. Add water and simmer until ingredients soften.
  3. Add curry sauce mix and stir until dissolved.
  4. Simmer until it thickens.
