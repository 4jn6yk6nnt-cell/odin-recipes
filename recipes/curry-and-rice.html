Curry and rice

Description

Curry and rice is a uniquely evolved national dish in Japan, made by stewing meat and vegetables with a blend of spices, thickening the sauce with flour, and serving it over white rice

Ingredients

  • Curry sauce mix 1box
  • Meat 500g
  • Onions 4
  • Potatos 3
  • Carrot 1
  • Oil 2 table spoon
  • Water 1400ml

Steps

  1. Saute ingredients until yhe onions are lightly browned.
  2. Add water and simmer until ingredients soften.
  3. Add curry sauce mix and stir untill dissolved.
  4. Simmer untilit thickens
