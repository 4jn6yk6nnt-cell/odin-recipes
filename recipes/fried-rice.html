Fried rice

Description

Fried Rice is a classic Chinese dish made by stir-frying cooked rice with ingredients like eggs, meat, and vegetables in oil, then seasoning it with salt, soy sauce, or other condiments.

Ingredients

  • Fried rice mix 1
  • Salt 1 teaspoon
  • Soy sauce 1 teaspoon
  • Pepper 1 teaspoon
  • Chilli oil 1 teaspoon
  • Eggs 3
  • Rice 300g
  • Thinly sliced pork 50g
  • Green onions 20cm
  • Oil 1 tablespoon

Steps

  1. Cut the pork into small pieces and finely chop the green onions.
  2. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat and cook the pork until lightly browned.
  3. Add the eggs and stir quickly, then add the rice and mix everything together.
  4. Add the fried rice mix, salt, pepper, and soy sauce, and stir-fry until everything is well combined.
  5. Add the green onions and stir-fry briefly.
  6. Turn off the heat, drizzle with chilli oil, and mix well before serving.
