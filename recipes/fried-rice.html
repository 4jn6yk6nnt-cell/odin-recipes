Fried rice

Description

Fried Rice is a classic Chinese dish made by stir-frying cooked rice with ingredients like eggs, meat, and vegetables in oil, then seasoning it with salt, soy sauce, or other condiments.

Ingredients

  • Fried rice mix 1
  • Salt 1 teaspoon
  • Soy sauce 1 teaspoon
  • Pepper 1 teaspoon
  • Chilli oil 1 teaspoon
  • Eggs 3
  • Rice 300g
  • Thinly sliced pork 50g
  • Green onions 20cm
  • Oil 1 tablespoon
