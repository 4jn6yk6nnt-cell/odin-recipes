Fried rice

Description

Fried Rice is a classic Chinese dish made by stir-frying cooked rice with ingredients like eggs, meat, and vegetables in oil, then seasoning it with salt, soy sauce, or other condiments.

Ingredients

  • Fried rice mix 1
  • Salt 1 teaspoon
  • Soy sauce 1 teaspoon
  • Pepper 1 teaspoon
  • Chilli oil 1 teaspoon
  • Eggs 3
  • Rice 300g
  • Thinly sliced pork 50g
  • Green onions 20cm
  • Oil 1 tablespoon

Steps

  1. Roughly chop the thinly sliced pork and sprinkle with a pinch of salt. Roughly chop the green onions . Mince the ginger . Beat the eggs.
  2. Add the pork and half of the green onions to a frying pan without adding any oil and cook over medium-high heat.
  3. Once the green onions start to brown, reduce the heat to medium-low and push the ingredients to the edge of the pan. Add the salad oil and ginger to the empty space, tilt the pan, and stir-fry until fragrant.
  4. Heat over medium-high heat, pour the egg mixture from step 1 over the oil, place the rice on top, and mix everything together with a wooden spatula.
  5. Add 1 teaspoon of chicken stock powder, 1/2 teaspoon each of salt and garlic paste, 1/4 teaspoon of coarsely ground black pepper, and two pinches of sugar, and stir-fry until the mixture is crumbly .
  6. Add the remaining green onions, pour the soy sauce around the edge of the pan, stir-fry briefly, and turn off the heat. Season with a little salt if needed, and drizzle with sesame oil (fragrant oil) and mix well.
